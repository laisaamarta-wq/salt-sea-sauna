# SALT — sea sauna & cold-water club

Concept website for a fictional sea sauna and cold-water club on the beach at Majori, Jurmala.
Design and art direction: Marta Jakovleva.

- One static page: `index.html` (inline CSS and JS, Lenis smooth scroll from a CDN, no build step).
- Signature moment: one scroll into "The walk in" pins the section and plays a short film,
  from the warmth of the sauna to the cold Baltic, then releases the page.
- Photos and films are hosted on a CDN and referenced by URL.

SALT is an invented brand. Prices, times and the booking form are demo content.
